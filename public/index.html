Gasoline & diesel vehicles · 12V basics

Before you touch anything

Park safely, apply the parking brake, and select Park (manual: neutral). Keep hands, clothing, and tools away from moving parts. Never run an engine in an enclosed space.

Stop and get help if you smell fuel, see smoke, leaking fluid, a swollen or damaged battery, or damaged wiring. Don’t use starting fluid, tap the starter, bypass wiring, or work under the vehicle. This guide does not cover EVs or hybrid high-voltage systems; use your vehicle manual or roadside assistance.
