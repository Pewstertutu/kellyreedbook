06 / A MEASURABLE START

First milestone: 100 helpful responses.

  1. Validate the live guide and get a qualified automotive review before wider promotion.
  2. Submit the sitemap through your verified Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools accounts. Sitemap URL: kellyreedbook.netlify.app/sitemap.xml. Ownership verification and indexing are controlled by those services.
  3. Publish one introductory post and one demonstration through your own accounts. Listen to feedback and correct confusing checks.
  4. Ask a small, relevant group of local partners for an honest review. Offer the flyer only after they find it useful.
  5. Review optional helpfulness counts and problem reports, then test one advertising channel with a budget you choose. Pause if visitors do not find the guide useful.

A helpful response means someone reported a useful next step. It does not prove a repair, unique visitor, sale, or attributed conversion. There is no guarantee of search ranking, reach, or revenue.
