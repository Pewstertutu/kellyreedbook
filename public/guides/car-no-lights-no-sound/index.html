THE ROADSIDE FIELD GUIDE

Car has no lights and no starting sound

A dark dash and no response call for a basic 12V power-supply inspection. A discharged battery, failed connection, or main power fault may be involved.

Start these guided checks →

Stay safe first

Park safely, apply the parking brake, and use your owner’s manual. Stop if there is smoke, fuel smell, a leak, or a damaged battery. Keep clear of moving parts. No starting fluid, starter tapping, wiring bypasses, or work underneath the vehicle. EV and hybrid high-voltage systems are outside this guide.

1. Check whether any ordinary light works

Observe a normal interior light or headlight without dismantling anything. Some working lights change which symptom path is most useful.

2. Look without touching

If the battery is safely accessible and undamaged, an obvious loose or corroded connection is useful to report. Do not handle corrosion, disconnect cables, or bridge terminals.

3. Get a power-supply inspection

Start with battery and connection tests. Do not remove high-current main fuses. Stop and get professional help if the problem followed a reversed jump-start connection.

Should I check the neutral safety switch first?

A starting interlock can prevent cranking, but normally does not explain loss of all lights. First establish whether normal 12V power is available.

Know when to stop

If you are unsure, save your observations and contact roadside assistance or a qualified technician. This educational guide does not confirm a failed part. No qualified automotive technician review has been verified.
